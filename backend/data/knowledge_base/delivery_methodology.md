> **SYNTHETIC / DEMO DATA** for the fictitious company NorthPeak Digital Partners.

# Delivery Methodology

## Engagement Phases
1. **Discovery & Scoping** (1-3 weeks): requirements workshops, current-state architecture
   review, success-criteria definition.
2. **Design** (2-4 weeks): target architecture, data model, integration design, security review
   gate before build starts.
3. **Build** (varies): iterative delivery in 2-week sprints, with a working demo at the end of
   each sprint and a backlog visible to the client sponsor.
4. **Stabilization & Handover** (2-4 weeks): performance testing, runbook creation, knowledge
   transfer sessions, and a defined hypercare period (typically 2-4 weeks post go-live).

## Governance
Engagements above $250K in scope (illustrative threshold for this demo) include a steering
committee with biweekly status reporting covering scope, schedule, budget, and risk register.

## Change Management
Scope changes are handled through a documented change-request process requiring sponsor sign-off
before schedule or budget commitments are adjusted.

## Tooling
Standard delivery tooling: Jira for backlog/sprint tracking, Confluence for documentation,
Slack/Teams for day-to-day communication, and a shared risk register reviewed weekly.

## Support & SLAs
NorthPeak offers post-go-live managed support with response-time SLAs (e.g., 4-hour response for
Severity 1 issues during business hours). NorthPeak does not currently offer a 24/7/365
follow-the-sun support model; after-hours coverage would need to be scoped as a custom addition.
