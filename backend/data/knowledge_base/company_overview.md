# Company Overview — NorthPeak Digital Partners

NorthPeak Digital Partners is a mid-sized technology consultancy founded in 2014, specializing in
enterprise digital transformation, applied AI, and cloud modernization. NorthPeak employs
approximately 420 people across three delivery hubs (Austin, Krakow, and Bengaluru) and serves
mid-market to large enterprise clients primarily in retail, financial services, and logistics.

NorthPeak's practice areas are organized into four groups: Technology Advisory, AI & Data
Engineering, Cloud & Platform Engineering, and Cybersecurity & Risk. Each engagement is staffed
with a blended team drawn from these groups depending on scope.

NorthPeak operates as a privately held company. Specific revenue and financial figures are not
disclosed publicly and are not represented in this demo knowledge base — RFP requirements asking
for audited financial statements or bonding capacity should be treated as requiring information
outside this knowledge base.

NorthPeak's stated mission is to help enterprise clients move from pilot-stage AI and cloud
initiatives to production-grade systems with clear governance and measurable outcomes.
