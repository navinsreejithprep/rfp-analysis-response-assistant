# Team Credentials

## Practice Leadership (illustrative personas)

## Staffing Model
NorthPeak staffs engagements with a mix of permanent employees and a small vetted network of
contract specialists for peak load. Client-facing roles (engagement lead, solution architect)
are always staffed with permanent NorthPeak employees; specialist contractor use is disclosed to
clients as part of the staffing plan.

## Training & Enablement
NorthPeak runs an internal quarterly enablement program covering new cloud services, security
practices, and applied-AI techniques. NorthPeak does not currently operate a public training
academy or public certification program for external participants.

## Language & Locale Coverage
Delivery teams operate primarily in English. NorthPeak has not documented multilingual
(non-English) client-facing delivery in past engagements; RFPs requiring native-language
delivery in a language other than English are not supported by this evidence set.
