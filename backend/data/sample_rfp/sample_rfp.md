# Request for Proposal: Enterprise AI & Cloud Modernization Program
**Issuer:** Meridian Retail Group (fictitious, demo)
**Response Deadline:** Proposals must be submitted within 30 days of RFP issuance.

## 1. Background
Meridian Retail Group is seeking a qualified technology partner to modernize its order-processing
systems, migrate core applications to the cloud, and introduce AI-enabled automation for document
and invoice processing across its store network.

## 2. Functional Requirements
2.1 The vendor shall demonstrate experience implementing AI-enabled workflow automation for
document or invoice processing in a retail environment. Evaluation criteria: relevance of prior
case studies, measured efficiency gains.

2.2 The vendor shall provide a conversational customer support assistant capable of citing source
documentation for every response it gives to a customer inquiry.

2.3 The system should support integration with common enterprise platforms such as Salesforce for
customer data synchronization.

## 3. Technical Requirements
3.1 The vendor must support modern web application development using React/Next.js and a
Python or Node.js backend.

3.2 The vendor must provide a data platform capable of supporting role-based access control and
data lineage tracking sufficient to support internal audit requirements.

3.3 The vendor should describe experience with mainframe modernization, including COBOL system
migration, if applicable to their practice.

## 4. Security & Compliance Requirements
4.1 The vendor must implement encryption at rest and in transit for all systems handling customer
data.

4.2 The vendor must provide a current, independently audited SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001
certificate for their own corporate environment. This is a mandatory submission requirement.
Evaluation criteria: certificate must be dated within the last 12 months.

4.3 The vendor must hold FedRAMP Moderate authorization or equivalent, given Meridian's plans to
eventually support public-sector retail partnerships.

4.4 The vendor should describe their approach to supporting GDPR data-subject-request workflows.

## 5. Implementation Requirements
5.1 The vendor shall describe a phased implementation methodology including discovery, design,
build, and stabilization phases, with a defined hypercare period post go-live.

5.2 The vendor must describe prior experience migrating an on-premises application estate of at
least 100 servers to a public cloud provider.

## 6. Operations Requirements
6.1 The vendor must provide 24/7/365 follow-the-sun production support with defined Severity 1
response-time SLAs.

6.2 The vendor should describe their change-management process for handling scope changes during
an active engagement.

## 7. Commercial Requirements
7.1 The vendor must submit audited financial statements for the past three fiscal years as proof
of financial stability. Evaluation criteria: minimum bonding capacity of $2M.

7.2 The vendor should describe their standard engagement governance model for programs exceeding
$250,000 in scope.

## 8. Experience & Credentials
8.1 The vendor shall provide at least two case studies of relevant prior engagements in retail or
adjacent industries, including team size and engagement duration.

8.2 The vendor should describe relevant staff certifications held across their cloud, security,
and AI/data practice areas.

8.3 The vendor must describe prior experience delivering client-facing services in languages
other than English, given Meridian's plans for future international expansion.
